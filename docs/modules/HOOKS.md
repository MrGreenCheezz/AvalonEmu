# Module hooks

Generated by `AlbionLocal.Server --modules-docs`; do not edit. API 1.0.

Every hook is a method to override in the class named here and a lambda registration under `Hooks`: `CombatScript.OnBeforeDamage` is also `Hooks.Combat.OnBeforeDamage(e => ...)`.

## ServerScript

The server's life.

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnStarted` | `ServerArgs` | The server has started (all modules loaded, listeners open). A module loaded later (a reload) does not get it. |
| `OnStopping` | `ServerArgs` | The server is stopping: last chance to save. Players are still in game. |
| `OnTick` | `TickArgs` | Every world tick (50 ms by default). Prefer `IModuleTimers` for anything periodic. |

## PlayerScript

Players entering and leaving the game and the zones, going down, completing goals. Bots do not fire these.

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnLogin` | `PlayerLoginArgs` | A player logged in: its first Join after the login (not a cluster change), after the client has the world. Messages work here. |
| `OnLogout` | `PlayerArgs` | A player's connection ended (logout, disconnect, timeout), not a cluster change. Still readable; messages go nowhere. |
| `OnEnterZone` | `ZoneArgs` | A player arrived in a cluster: every Join (the login's too, with `ClusterChange` false), after `OnLogin`. Id = the cluster. |
| `OnLeaveZone` | `ZoneArgs` | A player leaves its cluster (a cluster change or a logout), still on its map. Id = the cluster. |
| `OnKnockedDown` | `PlayerDownArgs` | A player was knocked down (by a mob, a player, a fall to 0 health). |
| `OnDied` | `PlayerDownArgs` | A player died (a lethal kill, the die button, a knockdown that ran out). |
| `OnGoalCompleted` | `GoalArgs` | A goal was completed: a bounty, an expedition, a corrupted dungeon. Id = its kind ("bounty", "expedition", "corrupted"). |
| `OnBeforeCommand` | `BeforeCommandArgs` | A typed command is about to run, whoever's and from wherever (the game's chat or the server console; a module's command too): `Cancel` stops it and the reason goes back as its error. |
| `OnFameGained` | `FameGainedArgs` | A player gained fame (kills, gathering, crafting, goals, a GM's or a module's grant), after it was added and the client told. A tome's fame, which only feeds the destiny board, is not in it. |
| `OnSilverChanged` | `SilverChangedArgs` | A joined player's silver changed (loot, the market, trades, costs, a GM's or a module's). The silver of market sales made while away comes in before its Join and is not told. |

## CombatScript

Every hit and heal, whatever caused it: cancel or change them in OnBeforeDamage and OnBeforeHeal. Limit a method with [ForId("T8_MOB")] to a target mob.

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnBeforeDamage` | `BeforeDamageArgs` | A hit is about to land, before anything of it has happened: `Cancel` makes it as if it never landed (no damage, no combat, no threat, invisibility stays; a spell's hit animation still plays), `Amount` changes it. |
| `OnAfterDamage` | `HealthChangeArgs` | Health was lost to a hit (damage shields already took their part). |
| `OnBeforeAbsorb` | `AbsorbArgs` | A damage shield is about to take a hit, once per shield: `Amount` changes what it takes, `Cancel` lets the hit through it. |
| `OnAfterAbsorb` | `AbsorbArgs` | A damage shield took a hit, once per shield: how much, what is left of it, whether it broke. Read only. |
| `OnHitAvoided` | `HitAvoidedArgs` | A hit or a spell did not land: `Reason` says why, `ProtectedBy` which effect stopped it (the DODGE spell, say). Read only. |
| `OnBeforeHeal` | `BeforeHealArgs` | A heal on a player is about to land: `Cancel` stops it, `Amount` changes it. |
| `OnAfterHeal` | `HealthChangeArgs` | Health was gained from a heal. |

## MobScript

Mobs spawning and dying, their loot. Limit a method or the class with [ForId("T8_MOB")] to a mob.

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnSpawned` | `MobArgs` | A mob was put on a map (a spawn point's spawn or respawn, a GM's, an event's, a module's). Fires for every mob of a cluster when it loads: keep it cheap. |
| `OnDied` | `MobDeathArgs` | A mob was killed (after its rewards, loot and the systems' own handling). Despawns (.mob kill) do not count. |
| `OnLootRolled` | `LootArgs` | A mob's loot was rolled, before the bag is made: `Items` may be changed (add, remove). |
| `OnBeforeThink` | `BeforeThinkArgs` | A mob's AI is about to run its update (each world tick, while players are on its map and the mob AI is on): `Cancel` skips the engine's AI for this update, so the module drives the mob itself (`MoveTo`, `CastSpell`). A walk keeps being sent; a mob left cancelled does not chase, swing, leash or finish its return home. Its spawn spells have run. Without [ForId] this runs for every mob of every populated map. |
| `OnBeforeAggro` | `BeforeAggroArgs` | An idle mob picked a player in its aggro range: `Cancel` keeps it from that target for now (it picks again at its next update, so this comes every tick while it is refused). Damage, threat and a fellow's alert start a fight without asking: `OnAggro` tells of every start. |
| `OnAggro` | `MobAggroArgs` | A mob's fight started: it had no target and has one now (its own pick, damage, threat, a fellow's alert); told at the next tick. Not a change of target during the fight. Not for mobs that do not fight (guards, decorations). |
| `OnBeforeLeaveCombat` | `BeforeLeaveCombatArgs` | A mob is about to give up its fight: `Cancel` keeps it in the fight, and it is asked again at its next update while the reason holds. |
| `OnLeaveCombat` | `MobLeaveCombatArgs` | A mob's fight ended without its death: it lost its targets or its leash and goes home, or the mob AI went off. Actions on the mob (a cast, a walk, a despawn) wait for the next tick. |
| `OnBeforeRespawn` | `BeforeRespawnArgs` | A spawn point's respawn time has come, its mob picked: `Cancel` keeps it away; the point tries again after another respawn time. Not the first spawn of a loading map. |

## SpellScript

The life of a spell. Limit a method or the class with [ForId("MY_SPELL")] to a spell (a mask with * and ? works too).

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnBeforeCast` | `BeforeCastArgs` | A player's cast passed the engine's checks and is about to start: Cancel refuses it. Not fired for mobs or bots. |
| `OnCast` | `SpellCastArgs` | A cast went through: energy paid, cooldown started, the hit still to come. Also fired for mobs (the caster is an `IMob`). |
| `OnCancel` | `SpellCancelArgs` | A cast under way was dropped before it finished: see `Interrupted`. Also fired for mobs. Read only. |
| `OnBeforeHit` | `SpellHitArgs` | A player's cast's hit lands: `Cancel` skips the spells.xml effects (a scripted spell's OnHit still runs). |
| `OnAfterHit` | `SpellHitArgs` | A cast's hit has been handled and its effects have run (a player's also when OnBeforeHit cancelled them), a mob's too: `UnitsHit` says how many it reached. Read only: Cancel does nothing here. |
| `OnBeforeEffect` | `SpellEffectArgs` | A spell is about to act on one unit: `Cancel` keeps all of it off that unit. Runs once per unit reached. |
| `OnBeforeDamage` | `SpellDamageArgs` | One health or energy change of a spell is about to happen, before defences and shields: change Amount, or Cancel to drop it. Also for mobs' spells. |
| `OnAfterDamage` | `SpellDamageArgs` | One health or energy change of a spell has happened: Amount is what was lost or gained. Also for mobs' spells. Read only. |
| `OnBeforeTrigger` | `SpellTriggerArgs` | A hit or a cast is about to set off a player's reaction (ENRAGE on a hit taken, a parry's riposte, a passive's proc): `Cancel` stops it. |
| `OnTick` | `SpellTickArgs` | One channel tick of a player's channelled spell. Not fired for bots. |
| `OnAreaCreate` | `SpellAreaArgs` | A spell area (spelleffectarea, a field on the ground) was created. Id = the spell that holds the area node. |
| `OnAreaTick` | `SpellAreaArgs` | A tick of a spell area, with the units inside. Id = the spell that holds the area node. |

## BuffScript

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnBeforeApply` | `BeforeBuffArgs` | Before a spell's timed effects go on a unit (cast spells, area effects, procs): Cancel keeps it off. |
| `OnApply` | `BuffArgs` | The buff went on (new, or re-applied: a stack more). |
| `OnStackChange` | `BuffStackArgs` | A buff's stacks changed (re-applied, charges consumed). |
| `OnTick` | `BuffArgs` | A tick of a buff's over-time effect (damage/heal over time, a pulse). |
| `OnRemove` | `BuffRemoveArgs` | The buff went off: expired, removed (a cleanse, a dispel, a module), or cleared (death, reset, knockdown). |

## ItemScript

Equipment, consumables, crafting, gathering. Limit a method or the class with [ForId("T4_MAIN_SWORD")] to an item.

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnEquip` | `EquipArgs` | An item went into an equipment slot (also every worn item at a login: `AtLogin`). |
| `OnUnequip` | `EquipArgs` | An item left an equipment slot. `Slot` is -1 when the item has already gone from the slot. |
| `OnWeaponSwap` | `WeaponSwapArgs` | The main-hand weapon changed (id = the new weapon; "" when the hand is empty now). |
| `OnBreak` | `ItemBreakArgs` | An item's durability reached 0 (death and knockdown wear, a gathering tool, a fishing rod). |
| `OnCalcPassives` | `PassiveArgs` | The worn items' passives are worked out again (the equipment changed): add stat modifiers with `Add` (pick one from `Stat`: `Stat.AttackDamage, `Stat.MagicSpellDamage, …). A filtered hook runs when that item is worn. |
| `OnUsed` | `ItemUsedArgs` | A consumable was used: one use from the inventory (potions, food, chests, maps…) or the potion/food slot. Id = the item. |
| `OnCrafted` | `ItemCraftedArgs` | Items were crafted at a station (or bought at an NPC shop, which is a craft), per batch. Id = the item. |
| `OnHarvested` | `HarvestArgs` | A harvest (gathering or a fishing catch) gave items. Id = the item. |
| `OnStationAction` | `BuildingActionArgs` | A station action was asked for (craft, repair, salvage, enchant, reroll, awaken), with whether the server accepted it. Id = the station's building. |
| `OnBeforeLootTake` | `BeforeLootTakeArgs` | A player is about to take an item out of a loot container (a mob's bag, a dead player's bag, a chest): `Cancel` leaves it there, the reason going to the player. Per item of a take-all. Silver piles are not items. Id = the item. |
| `OnChestLootRolled` | `ChestLootArgs` | A loot chest's loot was rolled at its first opening (per player on a personal chest), before its items are made: `Items` may be changed (add, remove). Id = the chest. |

## MountScript

Mounting and riding. Limit a method or the class with [ForId("T3_MOUNT_HORSE")] to a mount.

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnBeforeMount` | `BeforeMountArgs` | A mount was asked for and passed the engine's checks: Cancel refuses it (the client's mount bar aborts). |
| `OnMountStart` | `MountArgs` | The mount bar started filling. `Reason` is "mount", or "remount" at the player's mount object. |
| `OnMounted` | `MountedArgs` | Mounted (the bar is full). `GallopBuff` may be changed: the speed buff the mount's gallop applies. |
| `OnMountCancel` | `MountArgs` | The mount bar stopped before it was full. `Reason` is a short text for logs ("damage", "logout", "the player walks", …). |
| `OnDismount` | `MountArgs` | Off the mount: `Reason` "dismount", "knockdown", or "died" (its bar ran out). |
| `OnCalcMountDamage` | `MountDamageArgs` | A hit on the mount bar: `Amount` may be changed. |
| `OnMountSpell` | `SpellCastArgs` | A mount spell (the ridden mount's ability) was cast. |

## UnitScript

Health and energy changes, combat state, revives.

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnHealthChange` | `UnitValueArgs` | A unit's health changed by a hit or a heal (not by regeneration; not for a bot). Id = the mob; a player only reaches the handlers for everything. |
| `OnEnergyChange` | `UnitValueArgs` | A player's energy changed by a cast's cost or a spell's drain or gain (not by regeneration; not for a bot). |
| `OnEnterCombat` | `UnitArgs` | A player entered combat (with a mob or a player). |
| `OnLeaveCombat` | `UnitArgs` | A player's combat ended. |
| `OnRevive` | `UnitArgs` | A knocked-down player got up (the timer, a revive by an ally, a resurrection, a GM). |
| `OnReload` | `UnitArgs` | Replayed for every joined player and living mob right after this module was (re)loaded, so it can rebuild its per-entity state (data bags and entity timers are cleared at an unload). |

## MapScript

Maps being built and dropped. Limit a method or the class with [ForId("0204")] to a cluster.

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnLoaded` | `MapArgs` | A map was built: its objects and mobs are on it. A cluster's map comes again after an unload, and an instance (a dungeon floor, an expedition) has a cluster of its own. Id = the cluster. |
| `OnUnloaded` | `MapArgs` | A built map was dropped (empty for a while, or its instance closed): it is gone from `LoadedClusters`. Id = the cluster. |

## NetworkScript

Everything between a client and the server. Limit a method or the class with [ForId("CastStart")] to one operation or event.

| Hook | Args | What |
|---|---|---|
| `OnRequest` | `RequestArgs` | An operation arrived from a player, before its handler: `Reject` or `Respond` stop the handler. Keep them cheap: a client sends several per second. |
| `OnEvent` | `OutgoingArgs` | An event is about to go to one client: `Cancel` keeps it from that client, `Replace` sends another. Fires for every event of every client: register for one id where possible. |
| `OnResponse` | `OutgoingArgs` | A response is about to go to one client: `Cancel` keeps it back, `Replace` sends another. |
